Privacy policy

ReadSeal, the Confluence app published by OwlWorks. In force since 20 September 2026.

Who we are

The short version

  • ReadSeal runs inside your Atlassian site and sends nothing outside it.
  • It records who confirmed which version of which page, and when. That record is the point of the app.
  • It stores Atlassian account identifiers. It never stores names or email addresses.
  • OwlWorks has no way to read what the app holds for your site.

Our role and yours

ReadSeal is software you install in your own Atlassian site. The organisation that installs it decides which pages must be confirmed, by whom, how long the records are kept, and who may read them: that organisation decides the purposes and is the data controller. OwlWorks provides the software; it operates no server of its own for it and does not access the data the app holds for your site. Atlassian hosts and stores that data as part of its own cloud services, under your agreement with Atlassian.

Display names are read from Confluence when the app writes a campaign page, held in memory for at most ten minutes, and never written to storage. Email addresses are never read or stored.

The campaign pages the app writes are ordinary Confluence pages: they show the names of the people concerned, as your Confluence does everywhere else, and they belong to you.

Where it is stored

In Atlassian's Forge hosted storage, inside Atlassian's cloud, following the data residency of your Confluence site. ReadSeal declares no external host: no data is sent to OwlWorks or to any third party. There is no sub-processor besides Atlassian.

How long

  • While a campaign runs, and after it closes for as long as the administrator of your site chooses: always, or 1, 3, 5 or 10 years. Past that, the app erases its tracking data for that campaign.
  • The campaign pages in Confluence stay until you delete them, under your own retention rules.
  • Once a week, the app declares to Atlassian the account identifiers it holds. An account Atlassian reports as closed is erased from the app, wherever it appeared.
  • When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes its data after its own retention delay.

Your rights

If you were asked to confirm a page and want to know what is recorded about you, ask your own Confluence administrator or the manager of the campaign: they can see your record in the app and in the campaign pages, and they are the ones who decide what happens to it. OwlWorks cannot identify you in a customer's site, and has no access to that data; a request sent to us can only be forwarded to the customer concerned.

Deleting your Atlassian account causes the app to erase the identifiers it holds for you, through Atlassian's reporting mechanism described above.

Cookies and tracking

The app sets no cookie of its own and measures nothing. These pages set no cookie either and load nothing from a third party. They are served by GitHub Pages, which logs requests as any web host does.

Security

What the app stores, which permissions it asks for and what it refuses are described on the security page. To report a vulnerability, write to contact@owlworks.dev.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with its date, and a material change is announced in the app's release notes on the Atlassian Marketplace.
