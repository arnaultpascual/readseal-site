Support

Write to support@owlworks.dev. We answer within two business days, and usually the same day.

What helps us answer in one round

  • Your Confluence site address, for example yourcompany.atlassian.net.
  • The name of the campaign, and the page concerned.
  • What you expected, and what you saw. A screenshot of the screen is worth a paragraph.
  • The date and time, roughly, so we can look at the right moment.

Please do not send us personal data you do not need to: an account identifier or a campaign name is usually enough, and we never ask for a password or an API token.

Before writing

  • The documentation covers the whole life of a campaign.
  • The FAQ answers what people ask most often.
  • Known limits: 1,000 recipients per campaign, no Forge app in the Confluence mobile apps, and confirmations bound to the page version rather than to attached files.

Reporting a security issue

Write to security@owlworks.dev instead. See the security page.

Asking for a feature

Tell us the situation rather than the solution: what you are trying to prove, to whom, and what makes it hard today. That is how the useful half of this app got built.
